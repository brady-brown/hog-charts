Total minutes this lineup played together.
NetRtg
Net rating: points scored minus points allowed per 100 possessions with this lineup on the floor. Higher is better.
ORtg
Offensive rating: points scored per 100 possessions. Higher is better.
DRtg
Defensive rating: points allowed per 100 possessions. Lower is better.
