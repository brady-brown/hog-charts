Player Impact

RAPM (Regularized Adjusted Plus-Minus) isolates each player's effect on net efficiency per 100 possessions — independent of the box score — by solving for every player's value across all lineup combinations and adjusting for teammates and opponents. The On/Off columns show how the team's raw ratings move with the player on vs. off the floor. Box-Prior RAPM (the ⁺ columns) runs the same model but shrinks each player toward their box-score expectation (Hollinger Game Score) instead of toward zero — steadier in small samples.
